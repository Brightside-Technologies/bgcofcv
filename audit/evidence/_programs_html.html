College Scholarships

Since 1974, the Boys & Girls Clubs of Coachella Valley has helped hundreds of families send their children to college. Our program works to ensure that all members graduate from high school on time, ready for a post-secondary education and a career of the 21st century. To help students attain higher education, we offer college scholarships and support services in partnership with OneFuture Coachella Valley, and Alignment USA community. Our program is significant in that it is a renewable award. Once a student is on our program they are then eligible for continued funding throughout their college education.
